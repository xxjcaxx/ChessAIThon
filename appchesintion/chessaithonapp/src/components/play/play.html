ℹ️ How to play

  • Select Human or AI for each side.
  • For AI players, enter the server URL or pick a preset (Local / HF).
  • Adjust Simulations to control AI strength vs. speed.
  • Enable Suggest moves only to see AI hints without auto-play.
  • Press Start Game to begin; use Stop / Restart AI to pause or resume.
  • Click New Game to reset the board at any time.
