Here you can upload your own CSV/FEN files with chess scenarios. When you choose and resolve a scenario it will be stored in your browser. You can see and change your solution.
When you finish, you can export scenarios and solutions in CSV in order to train your AI model.
You can also load our default scenarios or provide a url for scenarios online.
You have to choose the best move in your opinion. You can change it if you want. You have to choose also the "value" of the move. Value is the expected provability to win the game in the scenario after the move. Value must be between -1 and 1. From i'm sure to loose or i'm sure to win. O is a draw.
